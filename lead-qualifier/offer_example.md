# What I'm Selling

## What we offer
We sell **RampUp**, a sales-enablement and onboarding platform that helps B2B
companies ramp new sales reps faster. It combines AI role-play practice, a
content/playbook library, and call analytics so new SDRs and AEs hit quota
sooner.

## The problem we solve / our value proposition
New sales hires typically take 4-6 months to reach full productivity. RampUp
cuts that ramp time by ~30% with structured practice and just-in-time coaching,
so revenue teams that are scaling don't lose pipeline to slow onboarding.

## Who it's for
- B2B SaaS and tech-enabled companies
- Roughly Series A to Series C / 50-1,000 employees
- Buyers: VP Sales, Head of Revenue/Sales Enablement, Sales Ops, RevOps
- Most valuable when a company is actively growing its sales team

## Hiring signals that suggest a fit
- Hiring **SDRs / BDRs / Account Executives** → expanding the sales floor, needs
  fast onboarding → STRONG fit.
- Hiring a **VP of Sales / Head of Sales / Sales Enablement / Sales Ops** →
  building go-to-market infrastructure → STRONG fit.
- Hiring **Customer Success** at scale → adjacent fit (onboarding playbooks).
- Only hiring engineering / no sales roles → weaker fit unless clearly scaling
  GTM soon.

## Anything else
- Sweet spot: companies hiring 3+ sales roles at once.
- Typical deal: $15-40k ARR. Sold to revenue leaders, not IT.
- Bad fit: tiny pre-seed teams, pure services/agencies with no repeatable sales
  motion, or large enterprises with an entrenched in-house enablement team.
